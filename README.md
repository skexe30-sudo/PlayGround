# Voidwake — Into the Void

A complete, no-build neon arcade survival game. You are the last light in a dead sector: outfly the swarm, collect nova shards, evolve your ship, and push your score as deep into the void as you can.

## Play

Open `index.html` directly, or serve the folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 3002
```

Then visit <http://localhost:3002>. The game is plain HTML, CSS, and Canvas — no dependencies or build step.

## Controls

| Action | Desktop | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | `WASD` or arrow keys | D-pad |
| Aim & fire | Move the mouse, hold left click | Hold **FIRE** (auto-aims) |
| Dash | `Space` | **DASH** |
| Pause | `Esc` / `P` | Pause button |
| Choose upgrade | `1` `2` `3` or click | Tap a card |

## The loop

- **Survive.** Hostiles enter from every edge and get faster, tougher, and more numerous as sectors advance.
- **Collect.** Destroyed ships drop nova shards. Move near them to pull them in and charge your next evolution.
- **Evolve.** Each nova charge offers three upgrades — fire rate, damage, hull, piercing rounds, dash cooldown, and more.
- **Chase the best.** Your personal best is stored locally in the browser.

Drones are fast and fragile; spitters keep their distance and fire tracking orbs; brutes are slow, armored, and hit hard. Every 22 seconds the sector advances and the pressure rises.
